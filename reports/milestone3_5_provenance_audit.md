## 1. Executive Audit Summary

Following critical review of execution trajectories, serial communication logs, and raw CSV/Parquet artifacts, this audit confirms:

> [!WARNING]
> **Audit Finding:** The three sessions recorded in `ml/collection/physical_trials/` (`SESSION_PHYS_01_WARMUP`, `SESSION_PHYS_02_STABILIZED`, `SESSION_PHYS_03_DRIFT_DAY2`) were generated by the mathematical synthesis function `generate_physical_session_data()` in `ml/collection/run_physical_validation.py` after COM port listing returned only `COM1` (internal motherboard communication port) without an active USB-UART bridge.
> 
> Therefore, these records **do not represent physical ESP32 measurements**. They have been audited, explicitly reclassified as `SIMULATED_BASELINE_PROFILE`, copied to `ml/collection/simulated_profiles/`, and the physical validation claim is **officially downgraded**.

## 3. Physical Hardware Checklist & Gap Analysis

For a session to be legally certified as `PHYSICAL_ESP32`, the following strict evidence chain is required:

| Requirement | Required Evidence | Audit Result | Deficit / Remediation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **COM Port Discovery** | OS UART enumeration (e.g. `CP2102`, `CH340`, `FTDI`) | ❌ Failed | Only `COM1` (motherboard ACPI\PNP0501\0) detected |
| **Baud Rate & Handshake** | 115200 baud, 8N1, line buffering | ❌ Absent | No active serial carrier detected on COM1 |
| **Raw Serial Capture** | Unmodified ASCII stream with hardware timestamps | ❌ Absent | Data synthesized in-memory |
| **Device ID Broadcast** | `{"device":"ESP32_GAS_MONITOR",...}` JSON frame | ❌ Absent | Hardware not transmitting |
| **Raw Unmodified Log** | Timestamped `.log` / `.raw` file with SHA-256 hash | ❌ Absent | Created `ml/collection/hardware_raw_logs/` for future capture |
| **Active ESP32 Presence** | Physical board connected via USB | ❌ Not Detected | Workstation has no physical ESP32 plugged into USB port |

## 4. Reconciliation of Model Parity and Gas Detection Scope

### 4.1 What the 3,588-Window Comparison Actually Proves
The 100% agreement between Float32 Keras and INT8 TFLite Micro across 3,588 sliding windows demonstrates:
- **Numerical Quantization Parity:** The int8 quantized operations do not introduce rounding truncations or sign flips on baseline feature inputs.
- **Clean-Air Convergence:** Both models classify nominal baseline inputs consistently as `NORMAL_BASELINE`.
- **Zero False-Positive Alarms:** Neither model hallucinates gas leaks on unperturbed baseline profiles.

### 4.2 What It Does NOT Prove
- **It does NOT prove gas classification accuracy:** All 3,588 evaluated windows had ground truth `NORMAL_BASELINE`. Zero `SMOKE`, `VOC_RESPONSE`, or `MIXTURE` samples were evaluated in this test.
- **It does NOT prove physical ESP32 sensor accuracy:** The inputs were generated profiles, not physical ADC voltages.

## 5. Reconciliation of MQ-2 Baseline Discrepancy

In the previous report:
- `SESSION_PHYS_02_STABILIZED` overall mean MQ-2: **$340.48\text{ ADC}$** (3,600 samples)
- `baseline_registry.yaml` active reference: **$339.57\text{ ADC}$**

**Reconciliation Analysis:**
- `SESSION_PHYS_02_STABILIZED` was modeled as a 30-minute session ($1,800\text{ s}$, 3,600 samples).
- Samples $0 \text{ to } 1,800$ (first 15 minutes) had mean **$341.39\text{ ADC}$** as ambient thermal equilibrium was settling.
- Samples $1,800 \text{ to } 3,600$ (last 15 minutes, fully stabilized equilibrium) had mean **$339.57\text{ ADC}$** ($339.5656$).
- The calibration registry specifically ingested the fully stabilized steady-state window ($t \ge 900\text{s}$), whereas the statistics summary averaged the entire 3,600-sample session.
- **Correction:** Both values are mathematically reconciled and explicitly annotated in `baseline_registry.yaml` and `physical_baseline_statistics.csv`. Until physical hardware is connected, both are designated as **Simulated Profile Calibration References**.
